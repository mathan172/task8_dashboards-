📝 Task 8 Summary – Simple Sales Dashboard Design
In this task, I created an interactive sales dashboard using Power BI based on the Superstore Sales dataset. The goal was to visualize sales performance across different regions, categories, and months.

Key steps included:

Importing the dataset and formatting the Order Date into Month-Year format.

Creating three visuals:

A line chart for sales over time,

A bar chart for sales by region,

A donut chart for sales by category.

Adding a slicer to filter the dashboard by Region.

Applying color formatting to highlight top-performing areas.

This dashboard enables business users to quickly understand trends and identify high-performing segments.

📊 Key Insights
West region consistently had the highest sales throughout the year.

Technology category generated the highest profits among all categories.

Sales peaked during November and December, showing a clear seasonal trend.

Central and South regions showed lower sales and may need focused strategies.
